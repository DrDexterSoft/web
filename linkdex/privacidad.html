Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 29, 2026

LinkDex: Link Manager (com.dextersoft.linkdex), developed by DexterSoft, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we handle your information and your rights regarding the data you store in our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, profile name, and a unique identifier (UID) to manage your account.
  • User Content: URLs, custom titles, tags, and groups in your library.
  • Images: Thumbnails stored locally on your device and, for premium users, also in the cloud.
  • Subscription Data: Subscription information managed by Apple App Store and Google Play.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Synchronization: To keep your library updated across your devices.
  • Offline Experience: Your data is stored locally on your device for offline access.
  • Account and Premium Features: To verify subscription status, manage premium features, and apply usage limits.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage) to manage accounts and data, and Apple App Store and Google Play to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions.

4. User Control and Data Deletion

You retain ownership of your data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app settings.

5. Security

We implement standard security measures, although no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
